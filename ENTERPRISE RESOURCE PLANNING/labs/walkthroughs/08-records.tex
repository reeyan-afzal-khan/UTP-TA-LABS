
\begin{labquestion}{1}{Map the actual handoff}
Draw lanes for Hiring Manager, HR and Candidate. Place vacancy approval, screening, interview, selection and offer in the responsible lane. Connect an output document or status to each next owner.
\end{labquestion}
\begin{labanswer}\textbf{Inspect the result.} Every transition has a sender, recipient and information item. A lane label alone does not explain a handoff.\end{labanswer}

\begin{labquestion}{2}{Add both decision outcomes}
After screening, draw a Yes path to interview and a No path to a rejection notification. After the offer, show acceptance and decline. Include the final owner for each outcome.
\end{labquestion}
\begin{labanswer}\textbf{Inspect the result.} All branches reach a clear end or a named rework step; no rejected candidate disappears from the process.\end{labanswer}
