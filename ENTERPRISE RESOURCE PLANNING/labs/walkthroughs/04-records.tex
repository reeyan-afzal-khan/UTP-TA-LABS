
\begin{labquestion}{1}{Keep the CRM record linked}
On the opportunity for Seri Iskandar Cycling Club, schedule a follow-up activity and enter the five-bike requirement in an internal note. Create the quotation from this opportunity.
\end{labquestion}
\begin{labanswer}\textbf{Inspect the result.} The opportunity links to the quotation, and both identify the same customer and salesperson.\end{labanswer}

\begin{labquestion}{2}{Inspect the quotation arithmetic}
Add Road Bike, quantity 5, unit price RM 1,000 and no discount for the classroom example. Check the untaxed subtotal before confirmation.
\end{labquestion}
\begin{labanswer}\textbf{Inspect the result.} Untaxed subtotal is RM 5,000. Record any configured taxes separately: with Malaysia's default 10\% tax, the quotation also shows RM 500 tax and RM 5,500 total. Expected CRM revenue is not a posted accounting revenue.\end{labanswer}
