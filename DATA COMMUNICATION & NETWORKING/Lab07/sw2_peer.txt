! ============================================================
! Lab 7 --- SW2, the far end of the Eth-Trunk
!
! An Eth-Trunk is an agreement between TWO switches. Configuring one
! end only is the usual reason a bundle never comes up, so build this
! side in step with manual_mode.txt and then lacp_mode.txt.
! ============================================================

system-view
sysname SW2

! ---------- Part 1: MANUAL mode (build this first) ----------
! Mirrors manual_mode.txt on SW1.
interface Eth-Trunk 1
 mode manual load-balance
quit

interface GigabitEthernet 0/0/1
 eth-trunk 1
quit
interface GigabitEthernet 0/0/2
 eth-trunk 1
quit

! ---------- Verify manual mode ----------
! display eth-trunk 1
!   Members are Up as soon as the cable is up. Nothing was negotiated:
!   this switch simply believes what it was told. Miscable one member
!   to a different switch and the bundle still forms --- a loop, with
!   no warning at all. That risk is why LACP exists.

! ---------- Part 2: rebuild in LACP mode ----------
! Run this only after observing manual mode. BOTH ends must change:
! manual on one end and LACP on the other leaves every member
! Unselected and the bundle down.
interface Eth-Trunk 1
 undo mode manual load-balance
 mode lacp-static
quit

! ---------- Deliberately DO NOT set lacp priority here ----------
! SW1 sets  lacp priority 100  (better than the default 32768), so SW1
! wins the system-priority election and chooses which links go active.
! This switch follows its decision.
!
! That is the lesson: port priorities configured on THIS switch have no
! effect while SW1 holds the election. Set them here and watch nothing
! change --- then swap the system priorities and watch control move.

quit
save

! ---------- Verify ----------
! display eth-trunk 1
!   Selected members here must match the Selected members on SW1.
!   The two ends agreeing is the whole point of the protocol.
!
! display lacp statistics eth-trunk 1
!   Frames sent AND received. Sent-only means the far end is not
!   speaking LACP --- check that SW1 really left manual mode.
